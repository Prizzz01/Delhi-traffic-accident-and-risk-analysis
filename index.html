Delhi Traffic and Accident Risk Analysis Report

Smart City Road Safety Initiative • Delhi Traffic Intelligence Project

Problem Statement

Delhi experiences a disproportionately high number of road accidents due to rapid motorization, extreme vehicle density, mixed traffic typologies, and severe seasonal environmental anomalies like winter smog and monsoon aquaplaning.

Civic authorities and traffic planning bodies often lack actionable data intelligence to pinpoint dangerous intersections and proactively prevent fatal outcomes.

Core Project Objectives

  • Identify accident-prone clusters and high-risk corridors in Delhi.
  • Analyze time-of-day crash distributions and peak hazard curves.
  • Evaluate environmental factors (fog, rain, smog) on severity.
  • Quantify vehicle vulnerability and correlate primary traffic violations.
  • Provide actionable evidence-based engineering and enforcement policies.

End-to-End Analytics Pipeline

1. Data Ingestion

500 accident logs capturing date, time, vehicle, weather, severity, injuries & deaths.

2. Cleaning & Prep

Handled missing values, standardized weather taxonomies, formatted 24h timestamps.

3. Transformation

Created risk scoring weights, peak-hour indices, casualty ratios, and corridor indices.

4. BI Modeling

Built DAX KPI measures, time-series curves, and multi-dimensional matrices in Power BI.

5. Web Interface

Engineered this standalone web interface with AI risk simulator and real-time filters.

Strategic Municipal & Traffic Management Recommendations

1. Automated Speed Enforcement on Outer Rings

Highways and low-density sectors exhibit fewer collisions but higher fatality rates due to excessive velocity. Deploy automated ANPR speed cameras along Lajpat Nagar & Punjabi Bagh flyovers.

2. Dynamic Winter Fog VMS Warning Systems

Fog and smog amplify accident severity by 45%. Integrate Variable Message Signs (VMS) with automated visibility sensors on arterial entryways (ISBT, Kashmere Gate, Dwarka).

3. Anti-Wrong-Side Physical Dividers

Wrong side driving accounted for 59 fatalities (highest among all violations). Install high-visibility median barriers and tire-puncturing one-way spikes at Saket and Karol Bagh market ramps.

4. Peak Evening Rush Signal Optimization

Maximum incident frequency concentrates between 18:00 and 21:00. Transition static traffic timers to adaptive AI-controlled signal phasing at major intersections.
